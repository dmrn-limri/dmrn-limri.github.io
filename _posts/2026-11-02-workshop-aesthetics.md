The second themed research event of LIMRI will focus on aesthetics with talks, discussion and a concert. We plan to bring speakers from philosophy, neuroscience/psychology, and (hopefully) technology.

* Monday 2 or Tuesday 3 November 2026
* Hosted by the SAS (School of Advanced Studies) Institute of Philosophy 
* Will take place in Senate House in central London
* Coordinated by Oded Ben-Tal, contact o.ben-tal@kingston.ac.uk if you have any questions or suggestions
