Week of April 27th, 2026, exact date TBC.

Hosted by C4DM at the QMUL Mile End Campus.

The first themed research events of LIMRI will be a one-day workshop that explores the many ways we can understand *expert performance in music*. Bringing together researchers and practitioners from fields including performance science, design engineering, music computing, and recording practices, the event will invite diverse perspectives on how musical expertise is developed, expressed, studied, captured, and modelled. The workshop will include 3-4 talks, a roundtable discussion, and a small concert. 

Confirmed speakers:
* [Prof Elaine Chew](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/people/elaine-chew) (King's College London)
* [Prof Andrew McPherson](https://andrewmcpherson.org/) (Imperial College London)
* [Dr. Amy Blier-Carruthers](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/people/amy-blier-carruthers) (King's College London + Guildhall School of Music and Drama + Royal Academy of Music)
