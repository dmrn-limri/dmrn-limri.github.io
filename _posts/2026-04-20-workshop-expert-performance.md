This one-day workshop explores the many ways we can understand *expert performance in music*. Bringing together researchers and practitioners from fields including performance science, design engineering, music computing, and recording practices, the event invites diverse perspectives on how musical expertise is developed, expressed, studied, captured, and modelled.

The workshop will be hosted by C4DM at the QMUL Mile End Campus.

Confirmed speakers:
* Prof Elaine Chew (King's College London)
* Prof Andrew McPherson (Imperial College London)
* Dr. Amy Blier-Carruthers (King's College London)
